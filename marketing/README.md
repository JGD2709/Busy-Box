# WonderBox social launch

Pre-launch content for Facebook and Instagram. Brand line: **One Box. Endless Possibilities.**

## Brand
Audience: South African parents and caregivers. Tone: cheerful, practical, curious, premium but approachable. Colours: purple #8550C9, blue #159BC4, yellow #F4C84A, white. Use original product photographs/renders rather than invented depictions of the product.

## Production specs
- Feed portrait: 1080 x 1350 (4:5)
- Square: 1080 x 1080
- Story / Reel cover: 1080 x 1920
- Keep critical words away from top/bottom Story interface areas.
- Use accessible contrast, large headlines, generous margins, concise copy.

## Asset workflow
1. Add approved WonderBox product photos to marketing/assets/ (do not use stock images to represent the product).
2. Produce each campaign creative with an editable source plus exported PNG.
3. Get approval for product features, pricing, availability, safety and age suitability before publishing.
4. Do not imply certification, developmental outcomes, compatibility, or launch dates that have not been verified.
